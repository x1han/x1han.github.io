Direct RNA-seq / Isoform Discovery

AIDRS

AI-Aided Isoform Discovery for direct RNA-Seq is a sequencing data-driven framework for full-length RNA isoform reconstruction and quantification from Oxford Nanopore direct RNA sequencing. It extends isoform discovery with coding potential prediction, translation start and stop codon identification, enhanced TSS/TES prediction, polyA-aware assessment, and annotation-guided filtering and rescue for low-abundance conserved isoforms.
